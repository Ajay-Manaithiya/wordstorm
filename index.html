How to play ⌨

Type any word you see falling on the screen. Completing a word clears it and scores points. A missed word costs one life. You have five lives.

The game gets faster every eight successful words. Each correct typed character contributes to WPM; accuracy tracks correct keystrokes versus all typed keystrokes. Keep your streak going for a combo bonus.

Hit Esc to pause. Try the goofy word pack for a silly round. Theme and colors can be changed under Customize branding. After the leaderboard is connected, you can submit your score with a public nickname. The share button copies your score and game link.

On mobile, tap the input field and type with your keyboard.
